# PROJECT VISION: The Tech Tree of Knowledge

> "Knowledge should be organized around both understanding and the ability to create."

---

## 1. The Core Problem

Human knowledge is vast, profound, and accelerating. Yet the systems through which humans encounter, acquire, and synthesize knowledge remain constrained by 19th-century academic structures:
- **Artificial Departmental Silos:** Physics is taught separately from Mathematics, which is taught separately from Computer Science, despite these fields being deeply symbiotic.
- **Arbitrary Chronological Grading:** High school classes (e.g., CBSE 9–12, US K–12) force learners into age-bracketed sequences rather than concept-mastery and competency-driven progression.
- **Passive Consumption vs. Creative Agency:** Educational content traditionally prioritizes rote memorization and passive consumption of textbook chapters over the active capability to design, simulate, code, synthesize, or build artifacts.

---

## 2. The Core Solution: The Tech Tree

The **Tech Tree of Knowledge** reimagines human learning as a dynamic, interconnected, and continuously expandable graph of knowledge modules. 

A learner does not start by enrolling in a rigid multi-year degree. A learner can start with a **creative goal**:
> *"I want to build an orbital mechanics simulator."*  
> *"I want to synthesize a solar cell dye."*  
> *"I want to design a microprocessor ALU."*

The system analyzes the target goal, identifies the necessary cross-disciplinary concepts, resolves the prerequisite chain, and sequences a custom **Learning Pathway** composed of independently maintained, peer-verified **Knowledge Modules**.

---

## 3. The Grand Architecture: Two Intertwined Trees

The complete master vision spans two foundational systems:

### System A: The Tree of Discovery (Civilizational Lineage)
- Reconstructs the historical trajectory of human invention and discovery.
- Maps how scientific instruments, experimental accidents, mathematical breakthroughs, and institutional paradigms influenced downstream technologies.
- Examples:
  - Optics & Lens Grinding $\to$ Telescopes $\to$ Galilean Astronomy $\to$ Newtonian Gravitation.
  - Maxwell's Equations $\to$ Hertzian Dipole Experiments $\to$ Radio Telephony $\to$ Modern Wireless Networks.
  - Boolean Algebra $\to$ Vacuum Tubes $\to$ Solid-state Physics $\to$ Transistors $\to$ VLSI Computing.
- **Implementation Status:** Architecturally planned and ontologically supported, but explicitly deferred beyond Version 1.

### System B: The Tree of Knowledge / Tree of Learning (V1 Core)
- Focuses on the direct transmission, verification, and application of knowledge.
- The fundamental currency is the **Knowledge Module**.
- Every module is sovereign, reusable, versioned, and tied to verifiable competencies and creative projects.
- **Implementation Status:** Primary target of Version 1.

---

## 4. Fundamental Guiding Principles

1. **Modularity is Non-Negotiable:** Every unit of knowledge must be independent, versioned, and self-contained. Improving one module must never silently break unrelated modules.
2. **Explicit Dependency Graphs:** Prerequisites are first-class edges in a directed acyclic graph (DAG). Circular dependencies are strictly forbidden and automatically rejected.
3. **Composability Over Duplication:** Pathways compose modules by reference. Content is never duplicated across multiple courses.
4. **Pedagogical Rigor Over Volume:** High quality, mathematically verified, empirically backed modules take absolute precedence over shallow, bulk-generated content.
5. **Radical Source Provenance:** Every claim, constant, derivation, and theorem must cite its lineage, publishing institution, peer-reviewed source, and copyright/OER license.
6. **Multi-Tiered Verification:** Unreviewed AI drafts must never be represented as established truth. The system enforces an auditable verification workflow.
7. **Decoupled Architecture:** Content and knowledge graphs are decoupled from rendering, UI frameworks, and storage backends.
